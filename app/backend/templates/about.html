About the Practice

Dr. Shehzad Khan founded Smile Care Dental Clinic with one belief: modern dentistry should feel less like a procedure and more like a conversation. From routine cleanings to complex restorations, every plan is walked through honestly, patiently, and priced transparently.

With a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) qualification and years of hands-on experience, Dr. Khan brings both clinical expertise and genuine compassion to every patient. The clinic uses modern equipment to ensure precision and comfort in every treatment.
